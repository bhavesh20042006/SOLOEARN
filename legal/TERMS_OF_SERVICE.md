## 3. Engagement Process

1. **Proposal**: SOLOEARN provides a written proposal detailing scope, deliverables, timeline, and pricing.
2. **Acceptance**: Client confirms acceptance in writing (email, WhatsApp, or signed document).
3. **Dataset Upload**: Client provides input data through the secure upload portal.
4. **Execution**: SOLOEARN processes the data in an isolated workspace.
5. **QA Verification**: Independent quality assurance checks are performed.
6. **Invoice**: A formal invoice with UPI payment details is issued upon completion.
7. **Delivery**: Deliverables are released after payment is verified.
