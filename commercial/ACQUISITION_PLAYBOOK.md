# SOLOEARN Client Acquisition Playbook: The 50/30/5/3 Campaign (M6.2)

To transform SOLOEARN from a verified software prototype into an actual income-generating enterprise, we execute a focused, manual client acquisition sprint targeting **three paid pilot customers**.

---

## 1. Experimental Funnel Targets

| Funnel Stage | Objective Target | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Identified Prospects** | **50** | Qualified businesses meeting our service profile. |
| **2. Personalized Approaches** | **30** | Thoughtful, individually tailored outreach messages. |
| **3. Serious Conversations** | **5** | Two-way dialogues discussing specific data challenges. |
| **4. Paid Pilot Customers** | **3** | Paying clients booking the ₹5,000 Business Analytics Pack. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Operating Rule:** Do NOT use automated mass-messaging bots, web scraping, or spamming scripts. Every prospective client must be identified manually, evaluated for business fit, and approached individually.

---

## 2. Ideal Prospect Profiles (Targeting the 50)

Look for small-to-medium businesses generating recurring transactional data without a dedicated in-house data analyst:

1. **D2C & E-Commerce Brands (15 prospects):**
   - Direct-to-Consumer brands on Shopify, WooCommerce, or Amazon India.
   - Selling apparel, packaged food, electronics accessories, or home decor.
   - Indicator: Active social presence, 50–500 orders/day, founder or marketing manager handles reports.
2. **Retail Chains & Multi-Store Outlets (15 prospects):**
   - Regional footwear, grocery, eyewear, or electronics stores with 2–10 locations.
   - Need weekly/monthly store-by-store sales and inventory comparisons.
3. **Wholesalers & B2B Distributors (10 prospects):**
   - Industrial supplies, auto components, or textile wholesalers.
   - Need customer payment reconciliation and product movement tracking.
4. **Chartered Accountants & Bookkeeping Practices (10 prospects):**
   - Small accounting firms overwhelmed by client statement cleaning and bank reconciliation.
   - Need clean CSVs and structured Excel summaries for tax filings.

## 4. Conducting the Conversation & Closing the 3 Pilots

When a prospect replies positively:
1. **Send the Sample Deliverable Pack:** Provide a sanitized demo package (e.g., the Q4 Sales Analysis package from `task_0469`).
2. **Confirm Scope & Privacy:**
   - Confirm file size (CSV up to 250,000 rows).
   - Reiterate that all processing is done 100% locally with zero cloud uploads.
3. **Set Commercial Terms:**
   - Price: ₹5,000 (Introductory Pilot).
   - Payment: 50% advance (₹2,500) upon intake, 50% upon delivery of verified package, or 100% upon delivery with invoice.
   - Guarantee: If the deliverable does not match the agreed specification and pass independent QA verification, the fee is 100% refunded.
4. **Issue Official Invoice:** Generate an official invoice from SOLOEARN (`INV-2026-XXXX.html`).
5. **Reconcile Payment:** Once the client transfers the funds via UPI/NEFT, import statement row or verify invoice payment in SOLOEARN to post the verified receipt to the financial ledger.
