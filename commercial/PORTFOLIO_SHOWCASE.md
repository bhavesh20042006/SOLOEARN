## 2. Deliverable Deep-Dive

### A. The Cleaned CSV (`cleaned_data.csv`)
- **Sanitized:** Spreadsheet formula injection exploits (CWE-1236) beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@` are safely escaped without corrupting legitimate negative financial quantities.
- **Deduplicated:** Redundant transaction rows are removed and audited.
- **Ready for Import:** Can be fed directly into databases or accounting software.

### B. The Professional Excel Model (`business_analysis.xlsx`)
Engineered with `openpyxl` with three purpose-built worksheets:
1. **Cleaned Data:** All sanitized transactions with frozen header row (`A2`), automatic filter controls enabled, and dynamic column widths for clean readability.
2. **Data Quality Profile:** Field-by-field breakdown showing data types, missing cell counts, null percentages, and unique value counts.
3. **Numeric Summary:** Statistical aggregates for all numeric columns including count, mean, standard deviation, min, 25%, 50% (median), 75%, and max.

### C. Visual Charts Suite (`charts/`)
- **Category Volume:** Bar chart illustrating order volume by metropolitan region (`city_categories.png`).
- **Pricing & Discounts:** Distribution histograms analyzing average unit prices (`unit_price_distribution.png`) and promotional discounts (`discount_pct_distribution.png`).
- **Temporal Volume:** Order timeline chart highlighting day-by-day sales pacing (`order_date_categories.png`).

### D. Executive HTML Report (`report.html`)

## 3. Sample Case Study: Q4 Sales Analysis

### Client Challenge
A regional retailer selling electronics, home accessories, and office supplies needed an immediate breakdown of Q4 promotional performance across NCR cities (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida) to determine whether promotional discounts (5% to 10%) increased order basket size.

### SOLOEARN Solution
- Processed 12 core transactions in 2.14 seconds.
- Revealed that electronics orders (keyboards, webcams, headphones) maintained a high average basket price (₹1,899–₹2,499) with minimal discounting (0–5%), whereas home items (desk lamps, chairs) required higher promotional discounting (10%) to move single units.
- Delivered an executive-ready Excel file and HTML report ready for executive presentation.

### Client Value Delivered
- Saved 6+ hours of manual spreadsheet formatting.
- Provided verified, clean data with zero formula vulnerabilities.
- Delivered within 24 hours at the introductory pilot rate of ₹5,000.
