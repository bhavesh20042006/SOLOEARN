## 2. Target Qualification Criteria (Finding 5 Genuine Businesses)

Select **5 real SMB businesses** operating in India that handle order or transaction CSVs manually:

| Criteria | Ideal Profile | Why This Fits SOLOEARN |
|---|---|---|
| **Business Type** | Direct-to-Consumer (D2C), Shopify/WooCommerce store, or Amazon/Flipkart seller | Generates weekly sales CSVs with messy columns, customer cities, and payment types |
| **Scale** | 30 to 300 orders per day | Too large to analyze by hand; too small to hire a full-time data analyst |
| **Current Pain** | Founder or operations manager spends 3–6 hours every Sunday in Excel | Immediate, high-urgency pain point solvable within 48 hours |
| **Contact Person** | Founder, Co-Founder, Head of Operations, or E-commerce Manager | Direct budget authority without complex corporate procurement cycles |

### Suggested Channels
1. **LinkedIn Search:** `"Founder" AND ("D2C" OR "E-commerce") AND "India"` (filter for 2–10 employees).
2. **Shopify Store Directory / Instagram D2C Brands:** Discover active Indian apparel, food, or electronics brands.
3. **Local Business Networks:** Local retail distributors or wholesale merchants in your city.

## 4. Model Pilot Agreement & Scoping Terms

To secure the first pilot smoothly, offer a tightly scoped, zero-risk pilot:

- **Service Scope:** **Tier 1: Business Analytics & Excel Pack**
  - Input: Client provides 1 transaction or sales CSV (up to 50,000 rows).
  - Deliverables:
    1. Cleaned, deduplicated CSV.
    2. Multi-sheet Excel workbook (`.xlsx`) with formulas, auto-filters, and KPI cards.
    3. Standalone HTML5 interactive report (`report.html`).
    4. 6 distribution charts in PNG format.
    5. Complete deliverable ZIP.
- **Turnaround SLA:** **48 hours** from data receipt.
- **Pricing:** **₹5,000 (ex-GST)** (Payment via UPI or direct bank transfer upon delivery and review).
- **Revision Policy:** 1 round of revisions included within 5 business days.
